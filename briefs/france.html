What your IT actually sees from France

  • IP geolocation. Orange, Free, SFR — all French residential ISPs resolve to "France" cleanly. M365 + Google Workspace flag country on first login.
  • MFA push location. Push approved from a French SIM (Orange, Free Mobile, SFR, Bouygues) adds France to the auth event.
  • Calendar timezone. CET/CEST is standard French metadata. Your meetings, Slack messages, Jira tickets all carry it.
France-specific gotcha: French fiber ("Fibre") is among the cheapest and fastest in the world — €30/mo for 1 Gbps symmetric is normal. Don't overpay for hotel wifi when an Airbnb with Fibre is right there.

Working hours overlap

France = CET (winter) / CEST (summer). So:

  • East Coast people: 9am ET = 3pm Paris. Workable through Parisian dinner.
  • Central Time people: 9am CT = 4pm Paris. Late-afternoon start.
  • West Coast people: 9am PT = 6pm Paris. Working through dinner.

Banking & streaming gotchas

Banking: France is low-friction for US bank fraud detection. Travel notice anyway. Most US debit cards work at French ATMs (BNP Paribas, Société Générale, Crédit Agricole all common). Capital One and Schwab fee-free.

Streaming: Netflix swaps to FR library. Hulu blocks. ESPN+ blocked. HBO Max blocked. Apple TV+ works. With IP at home, US libraries persist.

French equivalent: France Télévisions (free public TV), Canal+ (paid cable), MyTF1, M6 — all work on French IPs if you want to stream French content.

The "France-specific" things most people miss

  • Internet is exceptional and cheap. €30/mo for symmetric fiber is normal in cities. Even rural areas have decent ADSL.
  • French banks are GDPR-strict. Some US SaaS payment integrations get extra friction with French billing addresses.
  • EU GDPR creates the same audit-log artifacts as Italy — visible in some corporate logs.
  • Tax residency at 183 days like most countries. Long stays trigger France's notoriously detailed tax system — talk to an accountant.
  • August is a real thing. France pretty much shuts down. Bank customer service slow, mail slow, deliveries delayed.

Action plan before you fly

  1. Lock your device timezone to your home time zone before you fly.
  2. Travel-notice your bank(s).
  3. Look for "Fibre" listings when booking accommodation — French fiber is among the world's best, not all properties have it yet.
  4. Handle the IP layer — French IPs are unmistakable.
  5. Plan around August if your trip overlaps. Most French institutions slow down significantly.
